Catering Cost in Bangalore – Frequently Asked Questions

How much does catering cost in Bangalore?

Catering prices in Bangalore usually range from ₹350 to ₹800 per plate depending on menu, event type, and presentation style.

How much would it cost to feed 100 people?

For 100 guests, catering may cost between ₹35,000 and ₹80,000 based on food choices and venue setup.

What is the cheapest option for catering?

The most affordable option is a South Indian vegetarian buffet, starting from ₹250 per person.

How much does it cost to feed 30 or 50 people at a wedding?

For smaller gatherings, expect between ₹12,000–₹30,000 depending on your dishes and service preferences.

Is it cheaper to cater or cook myself?

Professional catering saves time and ensures quality, especially for large events. Cooking yourself is only cheaper for very small groups.

What is a good price for a buffet?

In Bangalore, a full buffet with starters, main course, and dessert costs around ₹400–₹700 per person.

How can I feed 100 guests on a budget?

Choose buffet-style catering, use seasonal ingredients, and limit desserts. ARS Caterers offers budget-friendly packages from ₹300 per plate.

What is the cheapest food to cater on a budget?

Vegetarian South Indian meals and biryani menus are the most affordable and popular catering options.

How much does it cost to feed 200 wedding guests in India?

Feeding 200 guests typically costs between ₹70,000 and ₹1.6 lakh depending on food selection and event scale.
